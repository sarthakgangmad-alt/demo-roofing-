Frequently Asked Questions

Get authoritative answers to common roofing questions regarding materials, timelines, warranties, and insurance in Toronto.

How does weather in the Greater Toronto Area affect roofing material choices?

Toronto experiences extreme freeze-thaw cycles in winter and intense humidity/heat in summer. Standing seam metal and natural slate offer the absolute best resistance to these cycles since they don't absorb moisture. Premium architectural shingles with synthetic underlayment are also excellent mid-range options that prevent ice damming.

What is the difference between a workmanship warranty and a manufacturer warranty?

A manufacturer warranty covers defects in the material itself (e.g., shingles losing granules prematurely). A workmanship warranty covers installation errors (e.g., improper nailing or flashing placement). Skyline provides both: direct Lifetime Workmanship protection and fully registered, factory-backed manufacturer warranties.

How long does a typical luxury roof replacement take in Toronto?

Most standard residential roofs take 2 to 4 business days. High-end natural slate or standing seam metal custom builds take 5 to 8 business days due to the specialized bending, cutting, and structural reinforcements required. We always monitor weather cycles closely to keep your home protected.

Do you offer financing options for premium roof replacements?

Yes, we partner with premier home improvement lenders in Ontario to offer 0% interest terms for up to 12 months, and low monthly repayment plans beginning at $149/month. Pre-approval takes 5 minutes online, is completely private, and does not impact your credit score.
